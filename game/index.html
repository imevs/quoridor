How to Play

  1. Goal: be the first to reach the opposite side of the board.
  2. On your turn either move your animal one tile, or plant a hedge.
  3. Move: click a glowing tile. You can jump over an adjacent animal; if a hedge is behind it, step diagonally.
  4. Hedge: hover the groove between tiles — a ghost hedge appears. Click to plant it. Hedges span two tiles.
  5. Hedges can't overlap or cross, and can never fully trap a player.
  6. Each player has 10 hedges (5 in a 4-player game).
