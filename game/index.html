    How to Play

    1. Goal: Be the first player to reach the opposite side of the board.
    2. Turn: Either move your pawn one tile, or place a wall in a groove.
    3. Move: Step to an adjacent tile. Jump straight over an adjacent opponent; if a wall blocks the jump, step diagonally.
    4. Walls: Walls span two tiles. They cannot overlap, cross, or completely trap any player.
    5. Inventory: 10 walls each in 2-player games, 5 in 4-player games.
